This project builds on the legacy of Dr. Jim Minstrell, whose work on learner cognition and the Facet Framework grew from decades of research with students in classroom settings and from enduring collaborations with teachers, educational researchers, and learning scientists. Supported by more than thirty years of funding from the NSF, the James S. McDonnell Foundation, and the U.S. Department of Education, this body of work continues to inform and inspire educational research, practice, and innovation. The website is created with the support of NSF ECR Core 2300382 awarded to the University of Washington.
